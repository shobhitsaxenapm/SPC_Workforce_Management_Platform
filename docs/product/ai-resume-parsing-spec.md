# AI Resume Parsing & Standardized Profile Extraction Specification
**Target Audience:** AI / ML Engineering Team  
**Document Purpose:** Product Requirements & Schema Definition for Automated Resume Intelligence  
**Document Length:** 2-Page Technical Product Spec  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Product Approach

### The Problem
Candidate resumes exist in millions of heterogeneous formats—multi-column layouts, varying date conventions, text walls, buzzword stuffing, and disparate fonts. In high-volume recruitment, recruiters and hiring managers waste 70% of their screening time navigating clutter to extract 5–6 core facts.

### The Solution: "Dual-Resume Standardized Profile"
The SPC platform operates on a **Dual-Resume Review** paradigm:
1. **Right Pane (Auditable Document):** The candidate's original resume (PDF/DOCX/Text) displayed verbatim as uploaded.
2. **Left Pane (Standardized AI Profile):** A clean, standardized executive profile generated by the AI extraction engine.

### Core AI Philosophy
- **High Signal-to-Noise:** Eliminate walls of bullet points, subjective adjectives, and 30+ keyword tag clouds.
- **Strictly Capped & Deterministic:** Every profile strictly adheres to a uniform structure so recruiters can evaluate any candidate in under 30 seconds.
- **Plain English:** AI summaries and evaluations must be written in crisp, recruiter-friendly language, avoiding impenetrable jargon.

## 2. Standardized Extraction Schema (What the UI Renders)

The AI engine must parse any arbitrary resume and output a structured JSON payload conforming to the following parameters:

| # | Parameter | Format / Constraint | Extraction Directive |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Full Name** | `string` | Candidate's legal/professional name. |
| **2** | **Current / Most Recent Role** | `string` | The most recent or active designation (e.g., `AI Product Manager`). |
| **3** | **Current / Most Recent Company** | `string` | The most recent organization or employer name. |
| **4** | **Total Experience** | `string` (e.g., `4+ Years`) | Total calculated years of relevant work experience across stints. |
| **5** | **Contact Details** | `email`, `phone`, `currentLocation` | Primary phone, email, and city/country (e.g., `Bangalore, India`). |
| **6** | **LinkedIn URL** | `string` (optional) | Direct profile link if present. |
| **7** | **AI Executive Summary** | `[string, string]` (Strictly 2 lines) | **Line 1:** Role & primary domain specialty.<br>**Line 2:** Execution track record & business outcome. |
| **8** | **Core Skills** | `string[]` (Strictly 5 items) | Top 5 primary competencies (aligned to JD if matched; otherwise top 5 core domain capabilities). |
| **9** | **Career Trajectory & Domain Strength** | `string` (1–2 sentences) | Macro synthesis of candidate's career progression and domain depth. |
| **10** | **Key Employment Stints** | `Array` (Capped at top 3) | Chronological recent 3 roles with dates, company, location, and a 1-line AI evaluation. |
| **11** | **Highest Qualification** | `qualification`, `institution`, `year` | Only the highest completed or active degree/diploma with institution and year. |
| **12** | **Compensation & Logistics** | `currentSalary`, `expectedSalary`, `noticePeriod` | Extracted if present in text/header; otherwise left for recruiter edit. |

## 3. Deep Dive: AI Synthesis Directives

### A. AI Executive Summary (Strictly 2 Lines)
Resumes typically feature vague 6-line objective statements full of buzzwords. The AI must distill the candidate into an easy-to-read **two-liner**:

- **Line 1 — Identity & Depth:** Who they are, total seniority, and primary domain focus.
  - *Format:* `<Role> with <Total Experience> in <Primary Domain / Technical Specialty>.`
  - *Example:* *"AI Product Manager with 4+ years of experience building AI automation tools, LLM workflows, and B2B SaaS platforms."*
- **Line 2 — Value Delivery & Impact:** Proven track record, what they actually delivered, and the business impact.
  - *Format:* `<Track record of delivering X outcome with Y team/execution style>.`
  - *Example:* *"Proven track record taking features from concept to launch with strong user adoption, reliability, and business impact."*
- **Guardrails:**
  - Must not exceed 2 lines.
  - Must not use generic filler words (e.g., "highly motivated self-starter", "synergistic thinker").

### B. Core Skills (Strictly Top 5)
Recruiters do not read 40-skill tag clouds. The AI must extract **exactly 5 skills**:
- **Scenario A (Candidate matched to a JD):** Extract the **Top 5 JD-Aligned Skills** from the resume that directly fulfill the job requirements (`Core Matched Skills`).
- **Scenario B (Unmatched / General Pool):** Extract the **Top 5 Core Competencies** that define the candidate’s primary capability tier.
- **Guardrails:** Filter out basic tools (e.g., MS Word, Zoom) unless specifically required. Focus on high-value domain and technical skills.

### C. Top 3 Employment Stints & 1-Line AI Evaluation
Resumes often list 6–8 bullets per role detailing everyday tasks. The UI displays only the **top 3 most recent stints**, and summarizes each stint into a single, punchy AI line.

- **Stint Fields:**
  1. `role`: Job title.
  2. `company`: Organization name.
  3. `location`: City / Remote status.
  4. `startDate` & `endDate`: Consistent format (e.g., `Nov 2025 – Present` or `Jul 2023 – Nov 2025`).
  5. `aiAnalysis` (**1-Line AI Evaluation**):
     - **Definition:** A single sentence stating the candidate’s core deliverable, ownership scope, and business/technical outcome in that role.
     - **Directive:** Answer *"What did they build or solve here?"*
     - **Good Example:** `Multi-agent orchestration and intelligent document processing pipelines.`
     - **Good Example:** `Creator risk screening, brand safety, and embedding-based identity resolution.`
     - **Bad Example:** `Responsible for attending daily standups and writing product documentation.` (Too task-oriented).
     - **Bad Example:** `Senior PM working at company.` (Repeats the title).

### D. Career Trajectory & Domain Strength
- A 1–2 sentence macro evaluation analyzing the candidate’s career progression:
  - *Example:* *"Shobhit Saxena demonstrates consistent career progression in AI Product Management with proven operational reliability across enterprise automation assignments."*

## 5. Engineering Constraints & Edge Cases

1. **Millions of Resume Formats (Robust Extraction):**
   - The extraction layer must support text extracted from multi-column PDFs, image-based scans (OCR), DOCX, and raw plain text.
   - Layout awareness is mandatory to prevent text from column A bleeding into column B.
2. **Missing or Incomplete Stints:**
   - If a candidate has only 1 or 2 stints, return only those without hallucinating extra stints.
   - If a candidate has 10 stints, pick the top 3 most recent / senior stints for the summary cards.
3. **Anti-Hallucination & Grounding:**
   - Extracted values (companies, degrees, dates, metrics) must be 100% grounded in the resume text.
   - If salary or notice period is not in the resume text, leave as empty string (`""`) so the recruiter can fill it during review.
4. **Deterministic Token Optimization:**
   - Keep prompt token budgets disciplined by extracting directly into the JSON schema in a single zero-shot or few-shot structured completion pass.
